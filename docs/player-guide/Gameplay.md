# Gameplay

You are dropped into a large city with a rifle. Somewhere in it is a gold scroll. Enemy soldiers patrol the streets, hear gunfire and movement, and shoot back.

## Objective

Find the gold scroll. It is marked on the minimap and its distance is shown at the top of the screen. Walk into it to win.

## Match flow

1. The intro screen shows the logo, the controls, and "Press Space or Click to Begin".
2. On start you fall into a random part of the city. Spawn protection runs from the match start and is refreshed to 3 s when you land.
3. Enemies patrol; if they see or hear you they chase, strafe, and fire.
4. Your health regenerates 4 HP/s after 6 s without damage.
5. Finding the scroll wins. Dying ends the match. `R` restarts either way with a freshly generated world.

## Survival notes

- Five rifle hits kill a soldier (65 damage each against 300 HP); soldiers regenerate out of combat, so finish them quickly.
- Enemy shots are blocked by the city geometry, so use buildings and stacked crates as cover.
- Crates can be grabbed and stacked to climb or to block lines of sight; they also absorb shots.
- The minimap shows the scroll and nearby enemies, which is the fastest way to orient yourself in the city.
