# Weapons and combat

RivalGrounds has a single weapon: a hitscan rifle with an M4 Colt viewmodel. There is no ammunition, reloading, weapon switching, or grenade.

## Firing

- Left click fires one shot per click.
- The shot is instant and travels from your eye along the crosshair.
- It damages the first enemy hit for 65, and pushes physical crates it passes through.
- Enemies have 300 HP and regenerate out of combat, so keep firing to finish them.
- Recoil is a visual kick: the crosshair gap widens for a moment.

## Taking damage

Enemy shots are also hitscan. A shot is stopped by buildings and crates. Each hit costs 16 HP and delays health regeneration by 6 s. Your health bar sits in the bottom-left; a red flash means you were hit.

## Feedback

- A white hit marker appears when your shot hits a soldier.
- Enemy health bars appear above engaged soldiers that are in view.
- The minimap shows threats around you.

## Tips

- Fight from cover: geometry blocks enemy fire.
- Enemies strafe once they are close, so lead your aim slightly when they are moving sideways.
- Break line of sight to drop out of combat; enemies return to patrol after a few seconds without seeing or hearing you.
