# Enemies

Enemies are armoured soldiers built from grey boxes with a red visor. They are worth understanding because they are the only threat.

## What they do

- **Patrol.** They walk between waypoints until they see you (a forward cone roughly 70° wide, up to 64 m) or hear you within 64 m.
- **Combat.** They face you, keep their preferred distance (chase beyond 18 m, back off inside 7 m, strafe otherwise) and fire every 2.0 s within 28 m.
- **Dead.** They fall over, turn dark red, and stop.

## Strengths and weaknesses

- 300 HP and health regeneration out of combat: they need sustained damage.
- Line of sight matters: walls, buildings and crates break it, both for their vision and for their shots.
- They do not collide with each other, so groups can bunch up — a good target for rapid fire.
- They lose track of you if you stay out of sight and out of earshot for about seven seconds.

## Reinforcements

The match starts with 14 soldiers. While fewer than 14 are alive, a new one is spawned every 14 s at 45–75 m from you. Soldiers you leave far behind (more than 170 m) are removed and replaced near you, so the fight follows you across the city. Clearing the map is therefore temporary; the objective stays the scroll.
